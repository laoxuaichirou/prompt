Issue

Fixes  [https://github.com/pytorch/pytorch/issues/194922] #194922


Summary

MPSGraph's padTensor: and padGradientWithIncomingGradientTensor: overwrite the copied input data at rank > 4, mostly with zeros and partly with other wrong values. The shape is correct, so nothing notices: F.pad(randn(1, 3, 9, 256, 544), (0,0,0,0,2,0)) is garbage in 79% of its elements. The boundary is in elements, not bytes: max(output last dim, product of output dims after the padded axis) >= 65536.


pad_out_template builds the graph at the operand's own rank, and only the trailing padding_size / 2 dims are padded, so this folds the leading dims into one batch dim, pads at rank <= 4 (measured correct at any extent), and reshapes back in-graph. It is in the shared template, so it also fixes reflection_pad3d and replication_pad3d, equally corrupt today, forward and backward.  [https://github.com/pytorch/pytorch/pull/194944] #194944 fixes constant padding only, and applies cleanly on top.

MPSGraph's padTensor:/padGradientWithIncomingGradientTensor: silently overwrite the
copied input data when the operand has rank > 4 and the output has a large inner
extent. The shape is correct and the padded border is correct, so nothing downstream
notices: F.pad(x, (0,0,0,0,2,0)) on a (1,3,9,256,544) float32 tensor returns garbage
in 79% of its elements. Most of the corrupt region reads exactly 0.0, the remainder
holds other wrong values, and the extent varies between identical calls in one
process.

The boundary is in elements, not bytes: corruption starts once
max(output last dim, product of output dims after the padded axis) reaches 65536.
It is bracketed to a single element for float32; int64 is clean at a trailing
32768 (262144 bytes) and corrupt at 65536.

pad_out_template builds the graph at the operand's own rank, so a 5D or 6D input
reaches the broken configuration. Only the trailing padding_size / 2 dimensions are
ever padded; everything in front of them is batch. Folding those leading dimensions
into one keeps the padding arithmetic identical while handing MPSGraph a rank <= 4
operand, which is correct at and far above the same inner extent. The output tensor
is left at full rank and the collapsed result is reshaped back inside the graph,
following the ND workaround already used by Linear.mm.

Because the fold happens in the shared template, this covers constant_pad_nd,
reflection_pad3d and replication_pad3d, forward and backward - all of which corrupt
today. A narrower alternative, routing only the bad shapes to
at::native::constant_pad_nd (extending the existing pad.size() > 6 fallback), would
fix constant padding alone and needs a magic 65536 threshold; the reflection and
replication paths have no such composite fallback.

Fixes  [https://github.com/pytorch/pytorch/issues/194922] pytorch#194922

Test Plan:

```
python test/test_mps.py TestPad -v
python test/test_mps.py -k pad
```

test_pad_5d_large_inner_dims covers constant, replicate and reflect on a 5D tensor
whose inner dimensions are exactly at the threshold, plus the pad-size-2 and
pad-size-4 variants, and checks the gradients as well as the forward output.
Measured before the fix (2.14.0a0+gitbf47514): the five cases are 50-100% wrong in
the forward direction and 67-83% wrong in the gradient. All 8 TestPad tests pass
after it, and the 143 tests matching -k pad are OK with 12 skipped.

## Issue

Fixes  [https://github.com/pytorch/pytorch/issues/194922] pytorch#194922

## Summary

MPSGraph's `padTensor:` and `padGradientWithIncomingGradientTensor:` overwrite the copied input data at rank > 4, mostly with zeros and partly with other wrong values. The shape is correct, so nothing notices: `F.pad(randn(1, 3, 9, 256, 544), (0,0,0,0,2,0))` is garbage in 79% of its elements. The boundary is in elements, not bytes: `max(output last dim, product of output dims after the padded axis) >= 65536`.

`pad_out_template` builds the graph at the operand's own rank, and only the trailing `padding_size / 2` dims are padded, so this folds the leading dims into one batch dim, pads at rank <= 4 (measured correct at any extent), and reshapes back in-graph. It is in the shared template, so it also fixes `reflection_pad3d` and `replication_pad3d`, equally corrupt today, forward and backward.  [https://github.com/pytorch/pytorch/pull/194944] pytorch#194944 fixes constant padding only, and applies cleanly on top.
